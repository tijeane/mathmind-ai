# Vision

## Purpose

The vision domain establishes MathMind's strategic direction. Documents here articulate mission, values, long-term goals, and the principles that guide product and organizational decisions across all other domains.

## Contents

This folder holds the mission statement, strategic pillars, value propositions, and competitive positioning. Vision documents are referenced by product, learning, and engineering teams to ensure alignment with MathMind's core purpose.
