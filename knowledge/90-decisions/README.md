# Decisions

## Purpose

The decisions domain preserves the rationale behind significant choices. Documents here record architecture decision records (ADRs), product decision records, and policy decisions so that future contributors understand why specific approaches were chosen and what alternatives were considered.

## Contents

This folder contains decision records with structured context, options evaluated, chosen approach, and consequences. Decision documents prevent repeated deliberation on settled questions and provide an audit trail for organizational learning.
